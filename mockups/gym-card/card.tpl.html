A counter card for nutrition coaches

Left at a front desk, this has a few seconds to tell an online nutrition coach it is for them — not the members, and not every trainer on the floor. The front names the reader, shows the roster triage they would actually get, and hands them a QR; the back asks for one client and one week. Print instructions are below the cards.

Sending it to a printer

The cards above are laid out at true print size, so what you see is what gets trimmed. Export straight from this page — the browser writes vector PDF, so type and the QR stay sharp at any resolution.

Export

  1. Press ⌘P / Ctrl P on this page.
  2. Destination: Save as PDF.
  3. Margins: None. Scale: 100%.
  4. Tick Background graphics.

You get a 2-page PDF — page 1 front, page 2 back. The page chrome and the dashed trim guides drop out automatically.

Measuring it

Every scan lands on the live landing page carrying ?ref=gym, so gym pickups show up separately in Vercel Analytics. Change it per venue — ?ref=lifetime-heights, ?ref=westheimer — and you learn which counters are worth restocking. Regenerate the QR whenever you change the parameter.

Two things to check before you order. Colour is RGB here and every printer converts to CMYK — the bright green on the back (#43DA7A) will come back a shade duller than it looks on screen, which is normal and still on-brand. And the card says Gardnr is free with no card; that is true only while BILLING_ENABLED is off. The day you turn the paywall on, these cards are wrong — so print a batch you can burn through, not a pallet.
